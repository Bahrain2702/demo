Our Painting Process

Quality results come from a careful process. We pay attention to every stage of wood painting to ensure a durable and professional finish.

STEP 01

下地確認 - Surface Inspection

We carefully inspect the condition of the wooden surface before starting the painting process.

  • Check the overall condition of the wood.
  • Ensure there is no rotten or damaged wood.
  • Inspect the condition of the existing paint.
  • Make sure the wood is not excessively damp.
  • Check the joints between wooden boards.

STEP 02

清掃 - Surface Cleaning

The wooden surface is thoroughly cleaned to remove contaminants and prepare it for the next stages.

  • Remove dust and dirt from the surface.
  • Remove moss or mold if present.
  • Clean oil stains and other marks.
  • Allow the surface to dry completely.

STEP 03

研磨 - Sanding

Sanding is carried out to smooth the wooden surface and remove loose or deteriorated layers of old paint.

  • Sand the surface of the wooden boards.
  • Remove peeling layers of old paint.
  • Smooth rough wood fibers.
  • Pay attention to corners and joints.
  • Remove sanding dust after completion.

STEP 04

下塗り - Primer Application

A suitable primer or wood sealer is applied to prepare the wooden surface and support proper paint adhesion.

  • Apply a suitable primer or wood sealer.
  • Use a brush to reach gaps and joints.
  • Apply evenly following the wood grain.

STEP 05

中塗り - Main Coat Application

The main color coating is applied carefully to achieve consistent coverage and a smooth appearance across the wooden surface.

  • Apply the main paint color.
  • Use dark brown or charcoal colors.
  • Follow the direction of the wood grain.
  • Maintain consistent coating thickness.
  • Avoid paint buildup between board joints.

STEP 06

上塗り - Final Coat Application

The final coating is applied to achieve an even color, consistent appearance, and the desired finishing quality.

  • Apply a second finishing coat if required by the product system.
  • Ensure an even overall color.
  • Check that no areas are missed.
  • Pay attention to board edges and ends.
  • Ensure the final gloss level meets the specifications.
