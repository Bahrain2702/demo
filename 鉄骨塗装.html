STEEL PAINTING PROCESS

Our Steel Painting Process

Quality steel painting requires careful surface preparation and professional coating techniques. We follow appropriate Japanese steel painting practices to protect steel structures against rust, corrosion, and environmental deterioration.

STEP 01

下地調査・確認 - Surface Inspection

Before painting begins, the steel surface is carefully inspected to determine its condition and the appropriate coating system.

  • Inspect the overall steel structure.
  • Check rust and corrosion conditions.
  • Identify peeling or damaged old paint.
  • Check surface defects and contamination.
  • Determine the required surface preparation.

STEP 02

脱脂・清掃 - Degreasing & Cleaning

The steel surface is thoroughly cleaned to remove oil, grease, dust, and other contaminants before surface preparation.

  • Remove oil and grease.
  • Clean dirt and dust.
  • Remove other surface contaminants.
  • Use suitable cleaning methods.
  • Allow the surface to dry when necessary.

STEP 03

ケレン・素地調整 - Rust Removal & Surface Preparation

Rust, loose paint, and unstable surface layers are removed to ensure proper adhesion of the protective coating.

  • Remove rust from steel surfaces.
  • Remove peeling and deteriorated paint.
  • Use appropriate grinding tools and equipment.
  • Prepare the surface according to the specified grade.
  • Check the surface condition after preparation.

STEP 04

除じん・養生 - Dust Removal & Masking

After surface preparation, dust and remaining particles are removed. Areas that should not receive paint are properly protected.

  • Remove dust and grinding residue.
  • Clean corners and steel joints.
  • Protect areas that should not be painted.
  • Check the surface before primer application.

STEP 05

下塗り（さび止め塗装） - Anti-Corrosion Primer

A suitable anti-corrosion primer is applied to protect the steel surface and improve the adhesion of subsequent coats.

  • Apply the specified anti-corrosion primer.
  • Cover edges, corners, and joints carefully.
  • Maintain the specified coating thickness.
  • Check for missed or uneven areas.
  • Allow sufficient drying time.

STEP 06

中塗り - Intermediate Coat Application

The intermediate coating is applied to build the protective film thickness and create an even coating surface.

  • Use the specified intermediate coating material.
  • Apply evenly across the steel surface.
  • Pay attention to corners and connections.
  • Maintain the required film thickness.
  • Observe the specified drying interval.

STEP 07

上塗り - Final Coat Application

The final coating is applied to provide the required appearance, durability, and additional protection against environmental conditions.

  • Apply the specified finishing paint.
  • Maintain consistent color and appearance.
  • Check for missed areas and paint defects.
  • Ensure the specified coating thickness.
  • Allow the coating to cure properly.

STEP 08

仕上がり検査 - Final Inspection & Quality Control

The completed steel coating is inspected to ensure that the work meets the required quality and project specifications.

  • Inspect the final coating appearance.
  • Check coating thickness when specified.
  • Check for pinholes, peeling, and uneven coating.
  • Inspect corners, joints, and edges.
  • Confirm the completed work meets specifications.
