Our Waterproofing Process

Reliable waterproofing starts with proper inspection and preparation. We carefully manage each stage of the process to help create a continuous, durable barrier against water.

STEP 01

下地調査 - Surface Inspection

We inspect the area to identify water leakage, cracks, deterioration, and other conditions that may affect the waterproofing work.

  • Check the condition of the roof, floor, or wall.
  • Identify cracks, gaps, and possible water entry points.
  • Check existing waterproofing layers for damage or peeling.
  • Confirm drainage routes and areas where water may collect.

STEP 02

高圧洗浄・清掃 - Surface Cleaning

The work area is cleaned to remove dirt and contaminants so the waterproofing materials can bond properly to the substrate.

  • Remove dust, dirt, and loose particles.
  • Clean moss, algae, and other surface contaminants.
  • Remove deteriorated or poorly bonded materials.
  • Allow the surface to dry as required by the material system.

STEP 03

下地補修 - Substrate Repair

Cracks, gaps, joints, and damaged areas are repaired before the waterproofing layer is installed.

  • Repair cracks and damaged substrate areas.
  • Seal joints, gaps, and penetrations where necessary.
  • Reinforce corners and connection details when required.
  • Prepare the surface to create a sound and even base.

STEP 04

プライマー塗布 - Primer Application

A compatible primer is applied to improve adhesion between the prepared substrate and the waterproofing material.

  • Select a primer suitable for the substrate and waterproofing system.
  • Apply the primer evenly over the specified area.
  • Pay special attention to corners, edges, and joints.
  • Allow the primer to dry according to the product instructions.

STEP 05

防水材塗布 - Waterproofing Layer Application

The selected waterproofing material is applied carefully to form a continuous protective layer across the designated surface.

  • Apply the waterproofing material in accordance with the specified system.
  • Maintain the required thickness and coverage.
  • Reinforce corners, joints, and detailed areas as required.
  • Avoid pinholes, gaps, and uneven application.

STEP 06

仕上げ・最終確認 - Finishing and Final Inspection

After the waterproofing layer has cured as required, the finished surface is checked to confirm the quality and continuity of the protective system.

  • Apply a protective topcoat when required by the system.
  • Inspect the surface for gaps, pinholes, or defects.
  • Check edges, joints, drains, and other detailed areas.
  • Confirm that the work has cured according to product specifications.
  • Perform a water test when appropriate and specified for the project.
